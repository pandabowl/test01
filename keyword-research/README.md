# Keyword Research: Sheffield Pottery (November 2026)

Full workbook: [`sheffield_keyword_research_2026-11.xlsx`](sheffield_keyword_research_2026-11.xlsx)
(93 keywords mapped to pages, plus on-site search, landing-page and competitor tabs).

**Data:** Shopify analytics for the last 365 days (sales, search-engine landing pages,
on-site search), the live collection catalog, and web-search spot checks on 2026-11-19.
**Not included:** measured search volume. "Est. demand" in the workbook is a directional
estimate. To replace it with real numbers, export Google Search Console (Queries + Pages,
last 16 months).

## Context

- Search engines drove **$3.44M of $9.59M** net sales (36%). Google alone was $2.98M.
- Revenue is concentrated in pottery wheels ($2.10M), kiln packages ($1.55M), kilns ($1.30M),
  glazes ($786K), clay ($782K) and pugmills ($488K). These clusters get the most attention below.

## Top findings

1. **Mid-fire glazes have no page.** "mid fire glaze" is the #1 on-site search (131 searches,
   2 clicks). "cone 5 glaze", "cone 6 glaze" and "mid range glaze" each got 11 searches and 0 clicks.
   The best sellers are labeled "High Fire" or "Cone 5-6", so store search surfaces niche items first.
   Competitors (Krueger, Clay-King, Seattle Pottery Supply) each have a brand-neutral mid-fire page.
2. **Duplicate pages compete for the biggest keywords:**
   - Pottery wheels: `/collections/pottery-wheels-sale` vs. `/collections/all-pottery-wheels`
   - Kilns: `/collections/ceramic-kilns-and-glass-kilns` vs. `/collections/all-kilns`
   - Clay: three collections all titled "Moist Pottery Clay"
   - Pottery wheel guides: three overlapping guides for one topic
3. **Empty or thin collections:** the Mid-Fire (cone 4-6) clay collection has 0 products. So does
   the Orton cone chart collection, which gets 4,803 search sessions a year. Starter Sets and Kits
   has 1 product.
4. **High-traffic informational pages lead almost nowhere.** The cone chart, Amaco Potter's Choice
   chart, Velvet chart, glaze layering page and raw materials dictionary together draw about 10,800
   search sessions a year and convert at under 0.3%. Adding "shop these" links is the cheapest fix.
5. **Missing brand-neutral collections** that competitors rank with: electric kilns, beginner
   pottery wheels, low fire glazes, cone 10 glazes, dinnerware-safe glazes, earthenware clay,
   and glass fusing kilns.
6. **Pugmills ($488K/yr) barely show up in search.** The collection converts at 0.11%, and
   Sheffield didn't appear in the spot check.
