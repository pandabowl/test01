Amaco Excel Kiln Model Comparison

Amaco Excel kilns are multi-sided, top-loading electric kilns in 8-, 10- and 12-sided sizes. Every current model has Amaco's Select Fire computer controller, and the 10- and 12-sided models we sell come with the EZ-Lift spring-loaded lid hinge. The chart below compares the current Excel models side by side. Specs are for 240-volt, single-phase power, the most common hookup in homes and schools.

Which Amaco Excel kiln should I buy?

  • First kiln for a home studioThe EX-232SF or EX-329SF. Both fire to cone 10, draw 33.4 amps on 240V and plug into a NEMA 6-50 outlet. The EX-329SF has thicker 3" brick. The EX-232SF has 2.5" brick and a slightly bigger 3.2 cu ft chamber.
  • Most popular Excel kilnThe EX-399SF. Its 12-sided, 27"-deep chamber holds 9.92 cu ft, and it has 3" brick and the EZ-Lift hinge as standard. It's rated to cone 8, a good fit for cone 5–6 glazes. It's our best-selling Amaco kiln.
  • Schools and classroomsThe EX-270SF (7.0 cu ft) or EX-365SF (6.57 cu ft, 3" brick). Both are 27" deep for full class loads. Check your building's power first: on 208V single-phase, the EX-270SF is rated to cone 6.
  • Cone 10 productionThe EX-1099SF. It has the same 28" × 27" chamber as the EX-399SF, but it's rated to cone 10 and wired directly to your electrical panel (60 amps on 240V single-phase).
How to read an Amaco Excel model number
  • EX = Excel. SF = Select Fire, Amaco's computer controller.
  • On most models, the first digit is the brick thickness: 2 = 2.5" brick, 3 = 3" brick.
  • The last two digits are the approximate capacity in cubic feet.

For example, the EX-270SF has 2.5" brick and a 7.0 cu ft chamber. The 12-sided EX-1099SF and EX-1266SF start with 10 and 12 instead. The EX-1099SF is the cone 10, direct-wire version of the EX-399SF, with the same 3" brick and 9.92 cu ft chamber.

Select Fire, EZ-Lift and 2.5" vs. 3" brick

Select Fire is Amaco's computer controller. It's standard on every current Excel kiln. EZ-Lift is a heavy-duty, spring-loaded lid hinge that makes the lid easier to open and close. It comes standard on every 10- and 12-sided Excel kiln we sell. If you already own an Excel kiln, see the EZ-Lift upgrade kits for the EX-247 and EX-270 and the EX-399, EX-1099 and EX-1266.

3" brick holds heat better than 2.5" brick, so the kiln reaches temperature more easily and cools more slowly. The trade-off is a slightly smaller interior: the 3" brick EX-353SF is 22½" across, while the 2.5" brick EX-257SF is 23½".

Every Excel kiln has Kanthal A-1 elements, a 7-foot power cord and a two-year warranty from Amaco. Most models also have reversible lid and floor slabs, which you can flip over to extend their life. To vent fumes from your kiln room, see the Amaco Master Kiln Vent.
