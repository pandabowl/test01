Amaco Excel Kiln Model Comparison

Amaco Excel kilns are round, top-loading electric kilns. Every current model has Amaco's Select Fire computer controller, and most come with the EZ-Lift spring-loaded lid hinge. Firing chambers run from 2.9 to 9.92 cubic feet, with 2.5" or 3" firebrick. The chart below compares the current Excel models side by side. Specs are for 240-volt, single-phase power, the most common hookup in homes and schools.

Which Amaco Excel kiln should I buy?

  • First kiln for a home studioThe EX-232SF or EX-329SF. Both fire to cone 10 and plug into a NEMA 6-50 outlet. The EX-329SF has thicker 3" brick. The EX-232SF has 2.5" brick and a slightly bigger 3.2 cu ft chamber.
  • Most popular Excel kilnThe EX-399SF. Its 28"-wide, 27"-deep chamber holds 9.92 cu ft, and it has 3" brick and the EZ-Lift hinge as standard. It's rated to cone 8, a good fit for cone 5–6 glazes. It's our best-selling Amaco kiln.
  • Schools and classroomsThe EX-270SF (7.0 cu ft) or EX-365SF (6.57 cu ft, 3" brick). Both are 27" deep for full class loads. Check your building's power first: on 208V single-phase, Amaco rates the EX-270SF to cone 6.
  • Cone 10 productionThe EX-1099SF. It has the same 28" × 27" chamber as the EX-399SF, but it's rated to cone 10 and wired directly to your electrical panel.

Select Fire, EZ-Lift and 2.5" vs. 3" brick

Select Fire is Amaco's computer controller. It's standard on every current Excel kiln. EZ-Lift is a heavy-duty, spring-loaded lid hinge that makes the lid easier to open and close. It's standard on most models and available on the EX-270SF and EX-365SF. If you already own an Excel kiln, see the EZ-Lift upgrade kits for the EX-247 and EX-270 and the EX-399, EX-1099 and EX-1266.

3" brick holds heat better than 2.5" brick, so the kiln reaches temperature more easily and cools more slowly. The trade-off is a slightly smaller interior: the 3" brick EX-353SF is 22½" across, while the 2.5" brick EX-257SF is 23½".

Every Excel kiln has Kanthal A-1 elements, a 7-foot power cord and a two-year warranty from Amaco. Most models also have reversible lid and floor slabs, which you can flip over to extend their life. To vent fumes from your kiln room, see the Amaco Master Kiln Vent.
