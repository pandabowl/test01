Cone Art Pottery Kiln Comparison

Every Tucker's Cone Art pottery kiln is a top-loading kiln that fires to cone 10, with double walls (2.5" firebrick plus 1" of block insulation) and a heating element in the floor. They come in round, square and oval shapes, from the 0.57 cu ft 119D test kiln to the 16.5 cu ft 4227D oval. The chart below compares every Cone Art pottery kiln we carry. Amps are for 240-volt and 208-volt single-phase power.

Which Cone Art kiln should I buy?

  • Test kilns and small spacesThe BX119D runs on 120 volts from a 20-amp outlet. For more room on a 30-amp, 240V circuit, see the BX1813D.
  • Most popular all-around kilnThe 2327D. Its 23.5"-wide, 27"-deep chamber holds about 104 cups a load, and it runs on a 60-amp breaker at 240V. It's our best-selling Cone Art kiln, most often as the GX2327D package with vent and furniture.
  • Platters and mixed loadsThe BX2818D for plates, platters and tiles, or a square 2322D SQ or 2327D SQ, which fit a 21" platter with cups in the corners.
  • Production and sculptureThe BX2827D (10 cu ft) or the ovals: the BX2336D, BX4222D and 16.5 cu ft BX4227D.
How to read a Cone Art model number
  • BX = Bartlett V6-CF keypad controller. GX = Bartlett Genesis 2.0 touchscreen controller with Wi-Fi. The kiln itself is the same.
  • Most model numbers give the interior size: the first two digits are the width and the last two are the depth, in inches.
  • SQ = square kiln.

For example, the GX2327D is 23.5" across and 27" deep inside, with the Genesis touchscreen.

Controllers, vents and packages

Lid lifter: every model of 4.7 cu ft or more (the 2318D and up) comes with Cone Art's patented lid lifter.

BX or GX: the V6-CF keypad stores 6 custom programs of up to 8 segments. The Genesis 2.0 touchscreen adds Wi-Fi monitoring from your phone, built-in diagnostics and a cost-per-firing estimate.

Order factory options with the kiln. Cone Art's integrated Orton VentMaster is wired to the controller, so the controller turns the vent on and off, and it can't be added after the kiln leaves the factory. A safety lid switch, which cuts power to the elements when the lid is opened, is a good choice for schools. Our Cone Art kiln packages pair a GX kiln with a factory-installed vent and a furniture kit.
