Evenheat Glass Kiln Model Comparison

Evenheat glass kilns come in two styles. Studio Pro kilns have Evenheat's Dual Access design: the chamber lifts off the floor so you can build right on the shelf from the front, and the lid also opens from the top. GTS kilns are traditional top-loaders. The chart below compares the Evenheat glass kilns we sell, using Evenheat's published specs for its standard build.

Specs are Evenheat's ratings for its standard build. Depth is the inside height of the chamber. Shelf is the full shelf Evenheat sells for that model. The Studio Pro 14 and 17 plug into a 120-volt, 20-amp (NEMA 5-20) outlet, and Evenheat also builds them for 240 volts; we sell the Studio Pro 14 both ways. Larger models can be built for 208V or 3-phase power by request, and the amperage changes with the voltage, so tell us your voltage when you order.

Which Evenheat glass kiln should I buy?

  • First glass kiln on household powerThe Studio Pro 17, Evenheat's best-selling personal-size kiln, takes a 16" × 16" shelf on a 120-volt, 20-amp outlet. The Studio Pro 14 is a smaller start with a 13" × 13" shelf.
  • Build in place from the frontThe Studio Pro 24, 28 or 41. Lift the chamber and you get a wall-free shelf for placing glass, raking and roll-ups.
  • Classic top-loaderThe GTS 18 or GTS 23. They're built entirely of firebrick, which stores heat early in the firing to smooth out temperature swings, then releases it slowly for calm annealing.
  • Large panels and productionThe GTS 2541 or GTS 41. Both take a 38" × 22⅝" one-piece shelf or a two-piece half-shelf set.

Small and specialty Evenheat kilns

The Studio Pro STP is Evenheat's smallest Studio Pro: an 8" × 8" × 4.5" chamber that fires to 1800°F on a standard 120-volt plug, for jewelry, one-off pieces and small parts for larger work. The V8 is built as a vitrigraph kiln for pulling cane. It has an 8" × 8" × 6.5" chamber, takes pots up to 7½" across, fires to 2300°F and plugs into a standard 120-volt outlet. You can also order it with the vitrigraph base. We also carry the Evenheat Copper 120V kiln.

Controllers: Evenheat's glass kilns offer the Icon, Icon+ and TAP II touchscreens or the push-button Rampmaster 3, depending on the model. Quiet Drive solid-state relays come standard on the Studio Pro and GTS kilns above.
