Evenheat Heat Treat Oven Comparison

Evenheat heat treat ovens for knife makers and metalworkers are front-loading, with doors that swing out to the side, away from you. They're built entirely of firebrick and come with Quiet Drive solid-state relays. The chart below compares the Evenheat heat treat ovens we sell, using Evenheat's published specs. For long blades, see the LB Series.

Which Evenheat heat treat oven should I buy?

  • No 240-volt outletThe KH 418, Evenheat's most popular KH oven. It reaches 2200°F on a standard 120-volt plug and has side-only elements like the LB. Expect about twice as long to reach temperature as a 240V oven.
  • Higher temperature and heavy loadsThe KO 18, Evenheat's most popular KO oven. Thicker firebrick and a 2350°F maximum give more speed and power for high-mass loads. The KO 27 is the deepest KO.
  • Swords and very long workThe KF Extreme ovens, 31.5" to 49.5" deep, with doors at both ends and the LB's side-only elements.
  • Shop work and temperingThe HT-1 or HT-2 for tool and die work, prototypes and color case hardening. For tempering, add an LT tempering oven.

Specs are Evenheat's ratings for its standard build. KH and LT ovens run on 120 volts only. The 240-volt KO, LB, KF and HT ovens can be built for 208V, 220V or 230V by request, and the amperage changes with the voltage, so tell us your voltage when you order. LT tempering ovens are not designed to sit on KH ovens.

Controllers and options

Evenheat's KH, KO, LB, KF and HT ovens are offered with the Icon or TAP II touchscreen or the push-button Rampmaster 3. LT tempering ovens come with the Icon. Options include Evenheat's Loud Alarm, an 80 dB alarm so you don't miss a quench, and blade fixtures.
