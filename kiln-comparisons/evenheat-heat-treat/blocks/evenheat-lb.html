Evenheat LB Long Blade Oven Comparison

Evenheat's LB Series heat treat ovens have heating elements in the side walls only, not the back wall, so a blade can use the full depth of the chamber without overheating at the back. Evenheat built them for knife makers, culinary and long-blade makers. All three models fire to 2200°F on 240 volts, open with a side-swinging door and come with Quiet Drive solid-state relays.

Specs are Evenheat's ratings for its standard 240-volt, single-phase build. Evenheat also builds LB ovens for 208V, 220V or 230V by request, and the amperage changes with the voltage, so tell us your voltage when you order. Controllers: Icon or TAP II touchscreen, or the push-button Rampmaster 3.

Tempering: an LT tempering oven (120V, to 1200°F) of the same depth sits on top of an LB, or works on its own on the bench. Longer work: the KF Extreme ovens share the LB's side-only element design in depths from 31.5" to 49.5"; see the Evenheat heat treat oven comparison.
