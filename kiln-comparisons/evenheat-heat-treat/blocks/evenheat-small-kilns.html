Evenheat Small Kilns for Metal Clay, Jewelry and Glass

These small Evenheat kilns all plug into a standard 120-volt household outlet. Check your clay or glass firing schedule against each kiln's maximum temperature. Specs are from Evenheat.

Model Loading Inside Capacity Max temp Power Hookup Best for
Studio Pro STP Front + top 8" × 8", 4.5" deep 0.17 cu ft 1800°F 120V, 12 amps NEMA 5-15 plug Jewelry, one-off pieces and small parts for larger work
V8 (standard base / vitrigraph base) Stackable 8" × 8", 6.5" deep 0.24 cu ft 2300°F 120V, 12 amps NEMA 5-15 plug Higher-temperature small work, and vitrigraph cane pulls with the vitrigraph base

The Studio Pro STP has Evenheat's Dual Access design: front and top access, with an optional 2" × 4" viewing window for glass firing up to 1800°F. The V8 is built in stackable sections; its vitrigraph base has a 2-3/4" hole for pulling cane and takes pots up to 7½" across, and its standard base lets you use it for other glass and ceramic work. Both are offered with the Icon controller; the V8 also with the Icon+, TAP II or Rampmaster 3.
