L&L DaVinci Kiln Comparison

DaVinci kilns are L&L's big square (X) and rectangular (T) top-loaders, with arched brick sides and a lid you lift with a winch. The sections plug into a floor-standing control panel, so you can add height later. They have 3" K23 brick, three zones and solid-state relays, are rated cone 10, and are wired directly to a disconnect.

Ratings are for 240V single-phase power. On 240V three-phase, the X3227-D draws 43.4 amps and the T3427-D 60 amps. L&L also makes taller DaVinci sizes and extra-power versions; ask us about them. Bowl count is L&L's estimate for 6" × 3" bowls.
