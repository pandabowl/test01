L&L Easy-Fire Kiln Comparison

Easy-Fire is L&L's main line of top-loading pottery kilns. Every current model has 3" K23 firebrick, a Genesis touchscreen with zone control (two zones in S and M models, three in T models), hard ceramic element holders, and a 6-foot cord with plug. They come in four diameters and three heights.

Ratings are for 240V single-phase power. On 208V single-phase, the e28M-3 is rated cone 8 and the e28T-3 cone 5. Every model except the e14S-3 also comes in a three-phase version, rated cone 10 with a NEMA 15-50 plug. Amps change with voltage, so check the spec table on each product page or tell us your voltage when you call. For frequent cone 6 firing or higher in a 28" kiln, L&L recommends the eQ2827-3 over the e28T-3.

Most Easy-Fire models also come as Easy-Fire kiln packages, with a Vent-Sure downdraft vent and a furniture kit of shelves and posts.
