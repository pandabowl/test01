L&L eFL Front-Loading Kiln Comparison

eFL kilns load from the front, so you set shelves in place instead of reaching down into the kiln. They have arched brick sides and top, a door with extra insulation and a safety switch, three zones, and 3" K23 brick. All three sizes are rated cone 10, are wired directly to a disconnect, and fit through a 31"-wide, 75"-high doorway before assembly.
