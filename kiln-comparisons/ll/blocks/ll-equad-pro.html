L&L eQuad-Pro Kiln Comparison

eQuad-Pro kilns are built for production potters who fire to cone 6 and above often. L&L gives them about 25% more power than an Easy-Fire, plus its Quad element system: thick, low-watt-density elements for longer element life. Both sizes have three zones and 3" K23 brick, are rated cone 10 for production firing, and are wired directly to a disconnect (no cord).

Ratings are for 240V single-phase power. On 208V single-phase, the eQ2327-3 draws 56 amps and the eQ2827-3 71.9 amps; both stay cone 10. The eQ2827-3 holds about 74 bowls (6" × 3") per load. It also comes as a package with a vent and furniture kit.
