L&L JH Crystalline Kiln Comparison

JH kilns are made for crystalline glazes, which need long holds at high temperature and carefully controlled cooling. L&L gives them extra power for fast heat-up, Quad super-heavy-duty elements, K25 high-temperature brick (2.5" sides and bottom, 3" top) for fast cooling, and Type S platinum thermocouples. All are rated cone 12 and are wired directly to a disconnect.

Model Inside (diameter × height) Capacity Max temp Amps Hookup Best for
JD18-JH 17.5" × 18" 2.6 cu ft Cone 12 (2400°F) 36.7 Direct wire Small batches and glaze testing
e23S-JH 23.3" × 18" 4.7 cu ft Cone 12 (2400°F) 47.9 Direct wire Wide, short loads of pots
JD230-JH 23.3" × 27" 7.0 cu ft Cone 12 (2400°F) 57 Direct wire Full-size production crystalline firing

Ratings are for 240V single-phase power. The JD18-JH and e23S-JH have two zones; the JD230-JH has three. Unlike most L&L kilns, JH kilns are not listed to UL 499.
