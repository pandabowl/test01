Which L&L kiln should I buy?

  • First kiln for a home studioThe e18S-3 or e18M-3. Both fire to cone 10 and plug into a standard NEMA 6-50 outlet. Only have a regular 120-volt outlet? The Plug-n-Fire reaches cone 10 on a 15-amp circuit.
  • Most popular all-around kilnThe e23T-3, our best-selling L&L kiln. Its 22.3" × 27" chamber holds about 38 bowls (6" × 3") per load, and it fires to cone 10. It also comes as a package with a vent and furniture kit.
  • SchoolsThe School-Master SM23T-3 for K-12 low-fire programs: e23T-3 size, simple controls and a five-year warranty. For cone 10 stoneware, L&L suggests the e23T-3 or e28S-3.
  • Production pottersThe eQ2827-3 eQuad-Pro holds about 74 bowls per load and has extra power and heavy Quad elements for frequent cone 6 to 10 firing. Prefer to load from the front? See the eFL2626 and eFL2635.
How to read an L&L model number
  • e = Easy-Fire, eQ = eQuad-Pro, eFL = eFL front-loading, SM = School-Master, JH = crystalline glaze kiln, X and T = square and rectangular DaVinci kilns.
  • In Easy-Fire and School-Master models, the number is the approximate inside diameter (14", 18", 23" or 28") and the letter is the inside height: S = short (18"), M = medium (22.5"), T = tall (27").
  • In eQuad-Pro, eFL and DaVinci models, the last two digits are the inside height in inches.
  • -3 = 3" thick firebrick.

For example, the e23T-3 is an Easy-Fire kiln about 23" across (22.3" inside) and 27" tall, with 3" brick.
