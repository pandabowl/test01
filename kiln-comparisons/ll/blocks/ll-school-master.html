L&L School-Master Kiln

The School-Master is L&L's kiln for K-12 schools. It has the same chamber as the Easy-Fire e23T-3, with a simpler single-zone Genesis Mini touchscreen and a five-year warranty. L&L designs it for low-fire work (cone 1) and limits it to cone 6. It runs on any voltage from 208 to 240.

Ratings are for 240V single-phase power; three-phase models come with a NEMA 15-50 plug. It holds about 38 bowls (6" × 3") per load. For cone 10 stoneware, L&L suggests the Easy-Fire e23T-3 or e28S-3 instead.
