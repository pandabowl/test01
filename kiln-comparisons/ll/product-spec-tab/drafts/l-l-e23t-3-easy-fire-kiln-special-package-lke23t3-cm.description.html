Included Vent Sure System
 By mounting the fan assembly away from the kiln L&L ensures the safety of the vent, keeps the vibration of the motor away from the kiln and ceramic ware, and keeps the heat of the kiln away from the fan motor for long trouble-free life. The fumes can’t get pushed out into your room through a leak in the duct. The patented downdraft technology from Orton improves kiln uniformity and improves glaze color.
