Nabertherm Kiln Model Comparison

Nabertherm kilns are made in Germany, carry a 36-month warranty, and are certified to UL 499 and CSA C22.2 No. 221 for use in North America. We carry two kinds: round and oval Top-series top loaders, and the front-loading N 200 chamber kiln. Every model fires to cone 10 and comes with Nabertherm's AC590 touchscreen controller. The chart below compares them side by side, using Nabertherm's U.S. specs: °F, inches and 240-volt power.

How to read a Nabertherm model name
  • Top = top loader, round or oval. N = chamber kiln: it loads from the front and heats from five sides.
  • The number is the chamber size in liters. A Top 60 holds 2.1 cu ft (60 liters); an N 200 holds 7.06 cu ft (200 liters).
  • /R = rapid, with higher-wattage elements that heat the kiln faster. /H = high temperature, for chamber kilns that fire to cone 13.

For example, the Top 60/R is a round top loader that holds 2.1 cu ft (60 liters), with the rapid, higher-wattage elements.

Which Nabertherm kiln should I buy?

  • First kiln for a home studioThe Top 60. On 240 volts it draws 15.2 amps and plugs into a NEMA 6-20 outlet on a 20-amp circuit, yet it still fires to cone 10. Want faster firings? The Top 60/R has the same chamber with higher-wattage elements.
  • Biggest plug-in kiln on single-phase powerThe Top 160. Its 5.65 cu ft chamber fires to cone 10 and takes a standard NEMA 6-50 plug on 240V single-phase power.
  • Schools and busy studiosThe Top 190 (6.70 cu ft, 26.7" deep) or the oval Top 220 (7.76 cu ft), whose 36.6"-long chamber takes wide platters and long pieces. Both are direct wire on single-phase power.
  • Production and frequent firingThe N 200 chamber kiln. It loads from the front, heats from five sides for very even temperatures, and holds up to 220 lb per load. It needs three-phase power.

Controller, options and larger kilns

AC590 controller. Every model here comes with Nabertherm's AC590 touchscreen controller. It stores up to 50 programs, has a cone firing assistant, saves firing data to a USB stick, and works with the free MyNabertherm app so you can check a firing from your phone over your Wi-Fi.

Top vs. Top/R. The /R models have a higher connected load (5.6 kW on the Top 60/R vs. 3.7 kW on the Top 60), so they heat up faster. Nabertherm builds them for frequent firing to cone 10. The trade-off is a bigger circuit.

Bottom heating. The Top 160, Top 190 and Top 220 can be ordered with optional bottom heating and manual zone control, for more even temperatures from top to bottom.

Need more room? Nabertherm also makes the larger N 300 (10.59 cu ft) and N 440 (15.53 cu ft) chamber kilns. Call us for details and lead times.

Nabertherm AC590 Kiln Controller

Our controller AC590 was developed and is produced within the Nabertherm Group. When developing controllers, our focus is on ease of use. The potter/ceramist can choose between 24 languages, the controller is custom-fit for each kiln model or the associated application. The AC590 is equipped with an integrated USB-interface for documentation of firing data (NTLog/NTGraph) and can also be used for cone firing in addition to free program input.
