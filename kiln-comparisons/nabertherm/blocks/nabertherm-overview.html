Which Nabertherm kiln should I buy?

  • First kiln for a home studioThe Top 60. On 240 volts it draws 15.2 amps and plugs into a NEMA 6-20 outlet on a 20-amp circuit, yet it still fires to cone 10. Want faster firings? The Top 60/R has the same chamber with higher-wattage elements.
  • Biggest plug-in kiln on single-phase powerThe Top 160. Its 5.65 cu ft chamber fires to cone 10 and takes a standard NEMA 6-50 plug on 240V single-phase power.
  • Schools and busy studiosThe Top 190 (6.70 cu ft, 26.7" deep) or the oval Top 220 (7.76 cu ft), whose 36.6"-long chamber takes wide platters and long pieces. Both are direct wire on single-phase power.
  • Production and frequent firingThe N 200 chamber kiln. It loads from the front, heats from five sides for very even temperatures, and holds up to 220 lb per load. It needs three-phase power.
How to read a Nabertherm model name
  • Top = top loader, round or oval. N = chamber kiln: it loads from the front and heats from five sides.
  • The number is the chamber size in liters. A Top 60 holds 2.1 cu ft (60 liters); an N 200 holds 7.06 cu ft (200 liters).
  • /R = rapid, with higher-wattage elements that heat the kiln faster. /H = high temperature, for chamber kilns that fire to cone 13.

For example, the Top 60/R is a round top loader that holds 2.1 cu ft (60 liters), with the rapid, higher-wattage elements.
