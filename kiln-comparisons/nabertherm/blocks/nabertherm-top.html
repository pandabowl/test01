Nabertherm Top-Loading Kiln Comparison

Nabertherm's Top-series kilns load from the top and have APM-wire heating elements in grooves all around the chamber walls. The Top 220 is oval; the others are round. Specs are for 240-volt, single-phase power.

Model Shape Inside Capacity Max load Max temp Amps Hookup Best for
Top 60 Round 16.0" dia. × 17.8" deep 2.11 cu ft (60 L) 66 lb Cone 10, 2408°F max 15.2 NEMA 6-20 plug, 20 A breaker Home studios with a 20-amp, 240V circuit
Top 60/RHigh-power version Round 16.0" dia. × 17.8" deep 2.11 cu ft (60 L) 66 lb Cone 10, 2408°F max 23.2 NEMA 6-30 plug, 30 A breaker Small studios that fire to cone 10 often
Top 160 Round 23.0" dia. × 22.4" deep 5.65 cu ft (160 L) 176 lb Cone 10, 2408°F max 37.7 NEMA 6-50 plug, 50 A breaker Active home studios and classrooms that want a plug-in kiln
Top 190 Round 23.0" dia. × 26.7" deep 6.70 cu ft (190 L) 176 lb Cone 10, 2408°F max 41.5 Direct wire, 60 A breaker Schools and busy studios, with room for tall work
Top 220 Oval 36.6" × 23.0" oval, 17.7" deep 7.76 cu ft (220 L) 150 lb Cone 10, 2408°F max 62.7 Direct wire, 80 A breaker Wide platters, long pieces and big mixed loads

Ratings are for 240V single-phase power. On 208V power the amps and hookups change: the Top 60 needs a NEMA 6-30 plug on a 30 A breaker, the Top 60/R a 6-50 plug on a 50 A breaker, and the Top 160 is direct wire. Every model except the Top 60 also comes in a three-phase version. Max load is Nabertherm's maximum charge weight. Specs are from Nabertherm's U.S. catalog and U.S./Canada manuals; tell us your voltage and phase when you call.

Top vs. Top/R. The /R models have a higher connected load (5.6 kW on the Top 60/R vs. 3.7 kW on the Top 60), so they heat up faster. Nabertherm builds them for frequent firing to cone 10. The trade-off is a bigger circuit.

Bottom heating. The Top 160, Top 190 and Top 220 can be ordered with optional bottom heating and manual zone control, for more even temperatures from top to bottom.
