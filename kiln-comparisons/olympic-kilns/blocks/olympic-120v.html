Olympic 120-Volt Kiln Comparison

These Olympic kilns run on household 120-volt power. The HotBox models and the Doll/Test kiln fire to cone 10 (2350°F). A kiln with a NEMA 5-15 plug fits a standard household outlet; a NEMA 5-20 plug needs a 20-amp outlet. Specs come from Olympic's current price list.

Which 120-volt Olympic should I buy?

  • Cone 10 glaze testsThe HotBox E or HB86E. Both plug into a standard outlet.
  • Taller small piecesThe HB89E (9" deep) or the Doll/Test kiln. Both have a hinged lid and fire to cone 10.
  • More room on 120 voltsThe 1214-120E (0.86 cu ft, cone 4) or the 129E (0.58 cu ft, cone 6).
  • Glass and ceramics on the roadThe Traveler has a lid element for glass, a folding lid and wheels. It fires to 2000°F.

All of these need a 20-amp breaker. A blank or bead collar adds 4.5" of depth to a HotBox (not the HB89E), but lowers its top temperature to 2000°F. Need cone 10 with more room? The 1414HE test kiln (1.4 cu ft) runs on 240 volts.
