Olympic DownDraft Gas Kiln Comparison

Olympic's DownDraft kilns are large-capacity gas kilns for propane or natural gas, rated to cone 10 (2350°F). The exhaust and intake are at the bottom, so they need a chimney or stack. Walls are 4.5" insulating brick with 1" ceramic fiberboard (fiber walls are an option), inside an angle-iron frame with a stainless steel exterior. Specs come from Olympic's current price list.

Which DownDraft should I buy?

  • First large gas kilnThe DD9 (9.2 cu ft) or DD12 (13.5 cu ft).
  • Schools and production studiosThe DD17 or DD20: 17.3 and 20.5 cu ft of setting space.
  • High volumeThe DD24, DD30 or DD40: up to 40 cu ft.

Setting space is the usable area inside the kiln. Every model also comes as a car kiln, and a vent hood adds 24" to the outside depth and height. Most DownDrafts need a 1" gas line; ask us about the DD9. Propane figures are gallons per cone 10 firing; Olympic says to buy a larger tank than that.
