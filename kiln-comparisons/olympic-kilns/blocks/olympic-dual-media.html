Olympic Dual Media Kiln Comparison

Olympic's Dual Media (DM) kilns fire both ceramics and glass. They're built with 3" brick and fire to cone 10 (2350°F) for pottery, and a lid element for glass fusing is turned on with its own infinite switch. Olympic builds the 23"-wide and larger models with a lid-lift assist. Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.

Which Dual Media kiln should I buy?

  • Home studioThe DM1818HE or DM1823HE: 2.63 and 3.29 cu ft, both on a NEMA 6-50 plug.
  • SchoolsThe DM2318HE (NEMA 6-50 plug) or the direct-wired DM2323HE.
  • Large glass piecesThe ovals: DM2523HE, DM3018HE and DM3023HE, up to 42" long.
