Olympic FL Front-Loading Kiln Comparison

Olympic's FL kilns are large-capacity electric kilns with a front door, so you load shelves standing up instead of reaching down. The number in the model name is the cubic feet (FL20E = 20 cu ft). Walls are 4.5" insulating brick with 1" ceramic fiberboard and a 1" air space, inside a heavy angle-iron frame. Every FL model fires to cone 10 (2350°F). Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.

Which FL kiln should I buy?

  • Studio productionThe FL8E, a 24" cube that Olympic lists for a NEMA 6-50 plug or direct wire, or the FL12E for 12" more height.
  • Schools and busy studiosThe FL17E or FL20E: 17 and 20 cu ft, direct-wired.
  • Large production and industryThe FL27E through FL53E: 27 to 53 cu ft.
  • Ready-to-fire packagesSeveral FL sizes come as complete FL packages.

Amps are for single-phase power at 240V / 208V. Large-capacity Olympic kilns can also be wired for three-phase or 480 volts. Car versions of the FL10E and larger roll the door and floor out on 6-foot tracks. Olympic requires solid-state relays on kilns shipped to CA, CT, IL, MA, ME, MN, NH, NY, RI and VT. Olympic also quotes custom electric and gas kilns for heat treating, DPF filter cleaning and other industrial work.
