Olympic Freedom Series Comparison

Each Freedom package is a cone 10 Olympic kiln with a furniture kit, a kiln vent and a Freedom Kit of spare parts: two elements, a thermocouple, a pint of mortar, a crimping tool and a wiring schematic. Every model has 3" brick and fires to cone 10 (2350°F). Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.

Which Freedom package should I buy?

  • Tests and small workThe Freedom 1414HE: 1.4 cu ft on a NEMA 6-30 plug.
  • Home studioThe Freedom 1823HE: 3.29 cu ft on a NEMA 6-50 plug.
  • Schools and community studiosThe Freedom 2323HE or Freedom 2327HE: 5.83 and 7 cu ft, both on a NEMA 6-50 plug.
  • Production pottersThe Freedom 2827HE (10 cu ft) or the oval Freedom 2527HE (12.8 cu ft), both direct-wired.
