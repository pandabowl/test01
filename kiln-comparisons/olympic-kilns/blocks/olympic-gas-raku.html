Olympic Gas Raku and Torchbearer Kiln Comparison

Olympic's updraft gas kilns run on propane or natural gas, use venturi burners and fire to cone 10 (2350°F). The Raku models lift the firing chamber with a hand crank for fast turn-around. The Torchbearer is a top-loading, stackable gas kiln: add rings for more depth. Specs come from Olympic's current price list.

Which Olympic gas kiln should I buy?

  • RakuThe 18 Raku for most studios, or the 23 Raku and 28 Raku for bigger pieces.
  • Reduction firingThe 1827G Torchbearer to start, or the 2327G Torchbearer for 7 cu ft.
  • Large loadsThe 2827G Torchbearer or 2831G Torchbearer: 10 and 11.67 cu ft, with 3" brick.

All of these need a 3/4" gas line. On propane, Olympic says the tank should be at least 5 gallons for the 1827G and 18 Raku, and 15 gallons for the 2327G, 23 Raku, 2827G and 28 Raku. In an enclosed space, Olympic says a gas kiln needs an exhaust hood vented to the outside.
