Olympic Glass Kiln Comparison

Olympic's glass kilns are made for fusing and slumping. In model names, GF means glass fusing and TLC means the kiln loads two ways: lift the top off, or open it like a clamshell. Specs come from Olympic's current price list.

Which Olympic glass kiln should I buy?

  • First glass kilnThe Champ or Champ XL. Both plug into a standard household outlet.
  • More room on 120 voltsThe 186GFETLC: an 18" square chamber on a NEMA 5-20 plug.
  • Larger panelsThe GF2ETLC (20" square) or GF3ETLC (25" square), on 240 volts.
  • Glass and potteryThe Dual Media kilns fire glass with a lid element and pottery to cone 10.
