Olympic Medallion Artist Series Comparison

Medallion Artist Series (MAS) kilns are Olympic's lower-cost cone 10 stackables. They have the same chamber sizes as Olympic's HE models, with 3" brick and a green electrical box, and every model fires to cone 10 (2350°F). Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.

Which Medallion kiln should I buy?

  • Home studioThe MAS1818HE draws 21 amps at 240V and plugs into a NEMA 6-30 outlet. The MAS1823HE adds 4.5" of height on a NEMA 6-50 plug.
  • Schools and community studiosThe MAS2323HE or MAS2327HE: 5.83 and 7 cu ft, both on a NEMA 6-50 plug.
  • Big loadsThe MAS2823HE or MAS2827HE: 28 1/4" wide, 8.34 and 10 cu ft. The MAS2827HE is direct-wired.
