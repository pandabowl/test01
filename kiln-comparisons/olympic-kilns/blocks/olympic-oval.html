Olympic Oval Kiln Comparison

Olympic's oval kilns give you a long chamber for platters, long sculpture and big loads. They're 30", 37" or 42" wide and 20" to 33.5" deep, with 3" brick and a lid-lift assist. Every model here fires to cone 10 (2350°F). Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.

Which Olympic oval should I buy?

  • Long pieces in a smaller spaceThe 2018HE or 2023HE: 30" long, 5.85 and 7.2 cu ft, both on a NEMA 6-50 plug.
  • Studio productionThe 2523HE or 2527HE: 37" long, 10 and 12.8 cu ft.
  • The biggest loadsThe 3027HE or 3031HE: 42" long, 17.7 and 20.5 cu ft, direct-wired.
