Olympic Kiln Model Comparison

Olympic Kilns has built kilns since 1971 and now makes them in Flowery Branch, Georgia. The stackable top-loading kilns in this chart are 18", 23" or 28" wide and 18" to 31.5" deep. They're built in sections, so you can take the kiln apart to move it or change its depth without removing the whole electrical box. The chart below compares the Olympic stackable kilns we sell, side by side. Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.

Which Olympic kiln should I buy?

  • First kiln for a home studioThe 1818HE. It fires to cone 10 and draws 21 amps at 240V, so it plugs into a NEMA 6-30 outlet on a 30-amp circuit. Have a NEMA 6-50 outlet? The 1823HE adds 4.5" of height (3.29 cu ft).
  • Schools and community studiosThe 2323HE or 2327HE. Their 23 3/8"-wide chambers hold 5.83 and 7 cu ft, they fire to cone 10, and both plug into a NEMA 6-50 outlet. Olympic says its H models are tough enough for continuous classroom firing.
  • Wide, flat workThe 2318HE or 2818HE. At 18" deep, you can reach the floor of the kiln easily, and the 2818HE gives you 28 1/4" of width for platters and wide sculpture.
  • Production pottersThe 2827HE or 2831HE: 10 and 11.67 cu ft of cone 10 space, direct-wired. Need more? Olympic's oval kilns go up to 20.5 cu ft.
How to read an Olympic model number
  • The first two digits are the interior width: 18 = 17.5", 23 = 23 3/8", 28 = 28 1/4". The last two digits are the interior depth in inches (23 = 22.5", 31 = 31.5").
  • H = high fire: 3" brick, rated to cone 10 (2350°F). The 18" and 23" models without an H have 2.5" brick and fire to cone 8 (2300°F).
  • E = an electronic digital controller. Some of our listings drop the E (2327H = 2327HE).
  • Prefixes: MAS = Medallion Artist Series, F = Freedom package, DM = Dual Media (adds a lid element for glass), FL = front-loading (there, the number is the cubic feet).

For example, the 2327HE is 23 3/8" wide and 27" deep, with 3" brick, a cone 10 rating and an electronic controller.

Medallion, Freedom and other Olympic lines

Medallion Artist Series (MAS) kilns have the same chamber sizes and cone 10 rating as the HE models above, with 3" brick and a green electrical box. They're Olympic's lower-cost line, in six sizes from the MAS1818HE to the MAS2827HE.

Freedom Series packages pair a cone 10 kiln with a furniture kit, a kiln vent and a Freedom Kit of spare parts: two elements, a thermocouple, mortar and a crimping tool. Sizes run from the Freedom 1414HE to the oval Freedom 2527HE.

Need something smaller? Olympic's 120-volt kilns run on household power, and the HotBox models fire to cone 10 from a standard outlet. The 1414HE test kiln (1.4 cu ft, cone 10) runs on a NEMA 6-30 plug. For other loading styles, see the FL front-loading kilns and the Dual Media kilns for pottery and glass.
