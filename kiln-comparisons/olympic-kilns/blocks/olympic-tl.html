Olympic TL Top-Loading Commercial Kilns

Olympic's TL kilns are large-capacity top loaders, built like the FL front loaders: 4.5" insulating brick with 1" ceramic fiberboard and a 1" air space, inside a heavy angle-iron frame. They fire to cone 10 (2350°F) and are direct-wired. Specs come from Olympic's current price list and are for 240- or 208-volt, single-phase power.
