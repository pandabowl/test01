Paragon Pottery Kilns

Paragon pottery kilns are designed to reach cone 8 - 10, from the small glaze testers to the massive studio models. Paragon pottery kilns have been proven by the test of time. Most top-loading models have a row of 2" blank firebricks in the top of the kiln. They are less susceptible to breakage than a grooved brick.
