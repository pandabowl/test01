Skutt Glass Kiln Model Comparison

Skutt glass kilns range from small FireBox kilns that plug into a household outlet to 27"-deep production kilns. All of them are top-loading and automatic, with a programmable digital controller. The chart below compares Skutt glass kiln models side by side. The FireBox kilns run on 115 volts. The GM kilns are listed at 240 volts, single-phase, the most common hookup in homes and schools.

How to read a Skutt glass kiln model number
  • GM = GlassMaster digital controller. GMT = the same kiln with the GlassMaster Touchscreen controller and built-in Wi-Fi.
  • The first digits are the number of sides (8, 10 or 12). The GM1414 is an oval.
  • The last two digits give the depth. "14" models are 13.5" deep, with elements in the lid and sides. "18" and "27" models are 18" and 27" deep, with side elements for firing several shelves at once.
  • -3 = 3" firebrick.

For example, the GM1018 is a 10-sided kiln, 18" deep.

GM kiln ratings are for 240V single-phase power. On 208V the amps change (the GM1014, for example, draws 39.1 A), and the GM1214-3, GM1414 and GM1227-3 also come in 3-phase versions with a NEMA 15-50 plug. Max temperatures stay the same. Shelf sizes are the shelves Skutt specifies for each model. Check the spec table on each product page or tell us your voltage when you call.
