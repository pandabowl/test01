Why glass artists choose Skutt

GlassFire Mode: no programming needed

Choose Fuse, Tack Fuse or Slump, pick a speed for the size and thickness of your piece, and press Start. The GlassMaster writes the schedule for you, and it works for most projects.

Your own schedules when you want them

Ramp/Hold mode stores up to 8 custom programs of up to 8 segments each. Active keys let you adjust a running firing: Anneal Now, Add Time and Add Temp.

Current sensing in every GlassMaster

Read the amperage of each section and the voltage under load right on the controller. A technician can troubleshoot over the phone, with no live wires and no wasted service call.

Zone Control

A thermocouple in each section lets the controller run each section on its own, evening out temperature from top to bottom. That's critical when you fuse on several shelves or anneal large blown vessels.

Sectional design

The kiln comes apart into sections, so it's easier to get down the basement stairs and easier to repair. If a section is ever damaged, you replace that section, not the whole kiln.

Questions we hear a lot

What's included in the package?

The Skutt GM818 kiln with its stand, stand feet and peep plugs, plus the matching furniture kit (3 × 15" × 16" × 5/8" shelves (8-sided); 12 × 4" posts; Bag of kiln wash). Lift-gate service is free.

What does free lift-gate service mean?

Kilns are heavy. When yours ships by freight truck, the driver uses a hydraulic lift-gate to lower it from the truck to the ground at your address, so you don't need a loading dock, a forklift or a crew of friends. It's free with this package.

Can I get a touchscreen controller?

Yes. Skutt's GlassMaster Touchscreen controller can be added when you order a new GM818. It's an upgrade at the time of purchase, not a retrofit. Call us at (888) 774-2529 and we'll add it to your order.

Which voltage do I need?

The GM818 comes in 240V or 208V, single phase versions. Pick yours from the options above. If you're unsure, call us and we'll walk you through checking your panel.

Can I fire pottery in this kiln?

GlassMaster kilns are built for glass and top out at 1800°F. That's perfect for fusing, slumping, casting and annealing, but too cool for most clay bodies. See Skutt's ceramic kilns if you want to fire clay too.
