Everything you need to start firing, in one order: the Evenheat RM II 2322 kiln, an Orton VentMaster downdraft vent and an Evenheat XL1 furniture kit sized for this kiln.

A 23.5″ studio kiln with a load-friendly 22.25″ depth, for mid-fire (cone 5–6) and low-fire pottery.

What's included

  • Evenheat RM II 2322 Cone 8 Kiln: 23.5″ diameter × 22.25″ deep (5.8 cu ft), fires to 2300°F / cone 8.
  • Orton VentMaster downdraft vent (120V): pulls fumes out through the bottom of the kiln and sends them outdoors. Venting gives more even temperatures, faster cooling and longer element life.

Specifications

  • Chamber: 23.5″ diameter × 22.25″ deep (5.8 cu ft)
