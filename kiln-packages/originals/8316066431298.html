Industrial-gauge Kanthal A1 elements are seated and protected in dropped, recessed firebrick grooves. The elements are easy to replace, because you can comfortably reach into the Paragon Iguana Kiln.

Potters and ceramists who struggle with top-loading potter’s kilns will enjoy the Paragon Iguana Kiln. The detachable door swings open wide on a heavy ½” steel rod with sealed bearings. A spring-loaded latch presses the door tightly closed. A recessed firebrick lip around the door seals in heat.

 

For your peace of mind, the Paragon Iguana Kiln includes a switch that shuts off power to the controller when not in use.
