Turn Key Package: 7 cubic feet capacity 23" Square Kiln. 22.5" Tall interior for easy loading!

The Cone Art GX2322DSQ Square Kiln Package includes a factory installed Orton Kiln Vent, Cone 11 rated High Alumina Furniture Kit, and the state-of-the-art wifi enabled Bartlett Genesis Touchscreen Controller.

Note: pictured generic furniture kit: this kiln uses square and rectangular shelves

People love the 2327DSQ so much that Cone Art decided to extend the line up. Now that same stacking space of square shelves is available in shorter models too. Not all pots are round…..and all kilns shouldn’t be either.

This top loader comes with all the standard features that set Cone Art apart: heavy duty floor element, double wall design, patented lid lifter system, multi zone control, and oversized swing out control panel. The 3 zones and floor element ensure even firing top to bottom even at cone 10!

The 2322DSQ has a full 7 cubic feet of stacking space.
As you would expect, the square shape is great for tiles and other boxy items. But the nice thing about a square kiln is that your posts are set out in the corners so you have lots more usable space for large round plates and platters! This kiln features the Bartlett Genesis Touchscreen controller for state-of-the-art access to your firings!
