ConeArt GX2827D: Large top Loading electric kiln
The Tucker’s Cone Art Kilns GX2827D is one of our most versatile and popular models. It is a true workhorse production kiln that has been designed to fire easily to cone 10 even with high mass loads like tiles.

  • 10 cubic feet
  • 28" across X 27" deep
  • Double wall Insulation
  • Kanthal A-1 Elements
  • 208 or 240 volts
  • 70 or 80 Amp Breaker required
  • CSA approved
  • New Lid lifter Design at NO EXTRA CHARGE
  • 3 Zone control at NO EXTRA CHARGE

It is a double insulated kiln with 2 ½" premium firebrick plus 1" of block insulation in the walls and 3" of brick in the lid and floor. This extra thick wall insulation ensures a cooler, safer jacket that will save you 32% in electricity costs when compared to a conventional 2.5" wall profile and 19.3% when compared to a 3" brick wall profile. It also gives longer element and electrical component life since the elements don’t have to work so hard to achieve the same heat work. That means big savings firing after firing, year after year.
