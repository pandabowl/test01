New from Cone Art Kilns, a multi sided electric kiln designed to use a square shelf. Shelves for the 2327D SQ are 21×21”, yielding more stacking space than it’s cousin the 2327D. This top loader comes with all the standard features that set Cone Art apart: heavy duty floor element, double wall design, patented lid lifter system, multi zone control, and over sized swing out control panel. This kiln will easily fire to cone 10 and gives you a full 8.4 cft of stacking space. Not all pots are round…..and all kilns shouldn’t be either.

This kiln is available with either the standard V6-CF keypad or the new Genesis touch screen. The standard model is the BX2327D SQ. The Genesis version is GX2327D SQ.
