HIGH FIRE PRODUCTION KILNS WITH QUAD ELEMENT SYSTEM

Kiln Package includes: Kiln, Genesis Touchscreen Controller, L&L Vent-Sure Downdraft kiln venting system, and furniture.

The eQuad 2827-3 is 10.2 cubic feet
28" diameter by 27" high (10.2 cubic feet). 3" K23 brick. Higher power than normal kilns. Includes panel circuit fuses. Direct power connection. Cone 10 for all voltages. Three thermocouples. No charge for 3-phase versions. C-MET-us is listed in the USA & Canada. Cone 10 for all voltages.


The L&L Kilns eQuad Pro Series kilns are designed for heavy usage at high-fire temperatures.
They are rated Cone 10 with plenty of power for firing cone 6 and higher everyday!
These kilns feature Quad Element Design. The quad elements are super heavy-duty elements which wrap four times in each 9” high section. Quad elements and included 3 zone control ensure even firing top to bottom throughout your kiln.
Equad kilns include stand, peep hole plugs, easy to understand manual, an L&L hard ceramic element holders.

Wire Connection: Direct Wire Connection (no cord)

The lid is spring loaded for easy opening. When open, rests at an angle away from the kiln so that the loading is easy and unobstructed.

The floor of eQuad series kilns are reversible extending the life of your kiln!

L&L hard ceramic element holders are included. These protect the bricks from bumps and chipping.
Control: The Genesis control includes a touchscreen interface and WIFI connectivity. The intuitive interface is easy to use and understand. A free phone app allows you to monitor your kiln remotely.
Warranty: Limited 3 year warranty.
