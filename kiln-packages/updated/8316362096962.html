    Product Description

    The Tuckers Cone Art Kiln Model GX4227D is a massive 16.5 cubic feet oval kiln with a full 42" width, 31" from front to back and a 27" depth. The GX4227D double wall ceramic kiln is designed for a busy production or sculpture studio. It is a double insulated kiln with 2.5 " of premium firebrick plus 1" of block insulation around the walls. The floor and lid are constructed with 3" firebrick and has an additional 1" of block insulation. The entire kiln, including the lid, is clad in a stainless steel jacket for extra protection and durability.

    The extra insulation in the double wall ceramic kiln ensures even firings, stops heat loss through the walls and lid, saves 30% in electricity costs, and lengthens element life.

    There are heavy-duty Kanthal A-1 elements in the walls and floor. This kiln makes firing up to cone 10 effortless even with heavy loads.

    The kiln is equipped with a Bartlett Controller with 3 Zone Control at NO EXTRA CHARGE which is mounted on an angled easy view panel.The Tuckers Cone Art Kiln Model GX4227D also comes with our patented LID LIFTER at NO EXTRA CHARGE.

    The Cone Art GX4227D oval kiln has all the features and more that you need in a professional kiln.
