Shimpo VL Whisper Pottery Wheel With Splash Pan

The Shimpo VL Whisper (Nidec-Shimpo VL-Whisper) is a quiet, direct-drive electric pottery wheel with a 1/2 HP (400 W) brushless motor, 100 lbs of centering capacity, a 14" wheel head and a 0-250 rpm speed range. It runs on a standard 115V household outlet, has removable legs for tabletop or storage use, includes a two-piece splash pan, and carries a 5-year Shimpo warranty. It suits home studios, classrooms and production potters who want a strong wheel without motor or belt noise.

The VL Whisper combines the direct-drive technology of Shimpo's RK-Whisper with the popular layout of Shimpo's Velocity wheels. It was the second model in Shimpo's Whisper series.

Key Features

  • Brushless DC direct-drive motor. A beltless, magnet-driven, electronically controlled motor that is smooth, powerful and maintenance free.
  • Very quiet. No belts and no vibration, so the wheel is close to silent while you throw.
  • Holds your set speed. The electronic controller keeps the speed you set under load, even at slow speeds with 100 lbs of clay.
  • Stops instantly. With no belt, the wheel head stops as soon as the pedal returns to stop, with no extra rotation.
  • Doubles as a banding wheel. The wheel head turns freely by hand at 0 rpm.
  • Reversible. Works for left-handed and right-handed potters.
  • Removable, adjustable legs. Take the legs off to store the wheel or use it on a tabletop.

Choose the VL-Whisper for the larger 14" wheel head, the remote pedal and removable legs. The Shimpo RK-Whisper has the same motor in a compact body with a fixed pedal and hand lever. The Shimpo VL-Lite is a lighter, belt-driven wheel for smaller work (up to 25 lbs of clay) at a lower price.

Frequently asked questions

How quiet is the Shimpo VL Whisper?

It is one of the quietest wheels you can buy. The direct-drive brushless motor has no belts and produces no vibration, so there is no belt or motor whine while you throw.

What is the difference between the Shimpo VL Whisper and the VL-Lite?

The VL Whisper is the stronger wheel. It has a 400 W direct-drive motor that centers up to 100 lbs on a 14" wheel head. The VL-Lite has a 100 W belt-driven motor that centers up to 25 lbs on a 12" wheel head.

What is the difference between the VL Whisper and the RK-Whisper?

Both use the same 1/2 HP direct-drive motor and center 100 lbs. The VL Whisper has a 14" wheel head, a remote foot pedal and removable legs. The RK-Whisper has a 12" wheel head and a fixed foot pedal with a hand lever.

Can I throw standing up?

Yes. The optional leg extension set raises the wheel to a standing height of 35-37".

Does it work with bats?

Yes. The 14" wheel head is drilled for bat pins.

Can I throw platters wider than the splash pan?

Yes. The leg extension set raises the wheel head above the splash pan to make room for wider pieces.

What power does it need?

A standard 115V, 60 Hz household outlet. The wheel draws up to 9.0 A under load.

What warranty does it have?

Shimpo covers the VL Whisper with a 5-year warranty.

Safety notes

Pacemaker warning: this product may be harmful to a person who has a pacemaker. Serious injury or death could occur. Consult a physician before using.

Acidic clays: highly acidic clays can damage the surface of the aluminum wheel head and cause premature oxidation and pitting. Use a bat between the wheel head and the clay whenever possible.
